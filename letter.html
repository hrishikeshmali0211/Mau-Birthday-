Happy Birthday, My Love 🤍

My dearest Mau,

Today is all about you, and I wish I could find the perfect words to tell you just how special you are to me. But somehow, no matter how many words I write, they never feel enough.

You have this beautiful way of making ordinary moments feel extraordinary. Your smile, your laughter, your little habits, the way you talk, and even the smallest things about you have become some of my favourite things in life.

I hope this birthday brings you all the happiness you deserve. I hope you keep smiling, keep dreaming, and keep being the wonderful person you are.

And whenever life gets difficult, I hope you remember that you have someone who cares about you deeply, believes in you, and wants to see you happy.

Thank you for being a part of my life. Thank you for the memories we have made, and for all the beautiful moments still waiting for us.

If I could make one birthday wish for myself, it would be to keep making more memories with you.

Happy Birthday, my beautiful Mau. I love you more than these words can ever say.

Forever yours,
Hrishikesh ♡
